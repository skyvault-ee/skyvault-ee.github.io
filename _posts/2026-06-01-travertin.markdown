The catalog is driven by an automated supplier feed import, so hundreds of products and their variants stay in sync without anyone retyping a price list. Pricing works per square metre or per piece depending on the material, checkout issues proper invoices instead of taking card payments, and transactional mail is DKIM-signed so order confirmations actually reach the customer.
